A specialist in protecting people, not just ticking boxes

I am Michael Marwa, a Gender and Development Specialist and SEAH Accredited Investigator with more than 10 years of experience across child rights, PSEAH, youth development, social justice, and sexual and reproductive health and rights. I work with NGOs, UN agencies, donors, and civil society organizations to strengthen safeguarding systems, advance child protection, and build institutional capacity at national, regional, and international levels.

As a qualified Social Audit Fellow and a SEAH Accredited Investigator trained by OSACO, I bring together policy analysis, psychosocial insight, and investigative expertise, including for emerging risks in digital environments.
